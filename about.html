Our Mission

Karishmaa Care Foundation has been working tirelessly for the past 9 years to uplift the underprivileged communities in both rural and urban Maharashtra.

What We Do

  • Conduct educational programs for underprivileged children
  • Health camps and awareness drives
  • Women empowerment workshops
  • Disaster relief and food drives

Our Vision

To build a compassionate and equal society where everyone has access to basic rights, education, and opportunities to grow.
